**Decision: #1 — Don't Wake the Dragon.** It wins on the five tie-break criteria the project owner defined (retention,
click potential, CrazyGames fit, viral potential, low development risk), it rides the single hottest loop on the
platform (sneak → grab → chase → escape; §2.3 of the market research) with a premise everyone already knows, and it is a
different genre from the sibling project.

## 4. The game

**One sentence.** You are a tiny thief in a sleeping dragon's treasure cave: grab as much gold as you dare, but every
step and every clink pushes the dragon closer to waking — leave in time or get chased out in flames.

**Core loop (30–90 s).**
1. Drop into the lair next to the exit tunnel. Gold is everywhere; the dragon snores in the middle.
2. Move = noise. Walking fast is loud, sneaking is quiet, picking up loot clinks, bumping into bones clatters.
   The **Wake meter** rises with noise and slowly falls while you stand still. Over time the dragon's sleep gets
   lighter (a soft time limit). Snores mask noise: moving *during* a snore is nearly free → a rhythm to learn.
3. The dragon telegraphs: 50 % one eye half-opens, 80 % it growls and the screen pulses, 100 % **it wakes**: roar,
   slow-motion, chase and fire breath. Reach the exit to escape with everything; get caught and the loot is lost.
4. Bank the haul → gold → upgrades → bigger hoards, new dragons, rarer artefacts.

**Push-your-luck.** The bag has a capacity and heavy bags slow you down; the exit is always open; the richest loot lies
closest to the dragon's head. "One more pile" is the whole game.

**Progression.**
- Six upgrades (5 levels each): Quiet Boots (step noise), Big Bag (capacity), Fast Legs (speed), Gold Magnet (pickup
  radius), Lucky Charm (artefact odds), Lullaby (an active ability: once per heist, hum the dragon back to sleep).
- Lairs with their own dragon and gimmick: Cave (green dragon, bones that clatter), Ice (blue dragon, slippery ice),
  Volcano (red dragon, lava trickles, lighter sleeper), Royal Vault (gold dragon, huge hoard, pressure plates).
  Unlocked with gold; each has a bigger hoard and a richer artefact table.
- Artefacts in five rarity tiers (Common → Legendary) with a Hoard Museum; completing a lair's set unlocks a thief skin.
- Thief skins (cosmetic), milestones ("Escaped the Ice Dragon", "10 000 gold in one heist", …).
- Daily Heist: the same seeded lair for everyone, once per day, best haul tracked.

**Shareable moments.** Escaping one step ahead of a fireball; getting caught at the tunnel; a legendary artefact
reveal; an absurd haul; the dragon's eye opening while you stand on its snout; the "Close call!" bonus.

**Why return.** Unfinished museum sets, the next dragon, a cheaper-than-it-looks upgrade, the daily heist, a best haul
to beat, skins.

**Monetisation (Full Launch only).** Midgame ad: requested when the player taps "Next heist" on the result screen (SDK
paces it; first one only after 3 min of play). Rewarded: "Keep loot" (the dragon sneezes you out with the bag) when
caught — once per session with a 5 s countdown — and "Double haul" every third escape. Every rewarded button has an
equally sized "Continue"/"Next heist" alternative and disappears under adblock / Basic Launch. (A daily ad chest was
considered and left out: the Daily Heist is the daily hook, and fewer placements keep the loop clean.)

**Controls.** Desktop: WASD / arrows (hold Shift to sneak) or move the mouse (distance = speed); Space / E = Lullaby.
Mobile: drag anywhere = virtual joystick (drag distance = speed), one ability button. Esc/P pause.

**Tech stack.** TypeScript + Vite + Canvas 2D (code-drawn art, no image assets), DOM/CSS UI, Web-Audio synthesised sound,
fixed 120 Hz timestep, seeded RNG (daily heist), CrazyGames SDK v3 (HTML5) loaded from the CrazyGames CDN. Target:
< 300 KB zipped, first frame < 1 s, 60 fps on a 4 GB Chromebook. No framework: Phaser/Pixi would add 400 KB–1.2 MB for
features a single-screen 2D game does not need.

## 6. Thumbnail concept

- **Focus:** a tiny grinning thief tiptoeing with a bulging sack of gold across the paw of a huge sleeping dragon,
  one dragon eye half open and looking straight at him. Gold coins and a glowing legendary orb in the foreground.
- **Composition:** dragon head fills the right 60 % (landscape) / top (portrait), thief lower-left in a spotlight of
  light from the tunnel; warm gold against dark cave teal so the silhouette reads at 200 px.
- **Text:** only the title, stacked in two or three lines in the game's display font with a gold outline.
- **Emotion:** "he is about to get caught" — curiosity + humour, honest to the gameplay (this exact moment happens in
  every run).
- Three variants with the same elements: 16:9 1920×1080, 2:3 800×1200, 1:1 800×800, rendered from the game's own art.
